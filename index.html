Oct 15 to Oct 21. Arrows compare with the week before (Oct 8 to Oct 14).

This week at a glance

  • How things are going. 6 leads reached the CRM this week at $24.40 each, up from 4 at $34.26 the week before, on $146.42 of spend. Meta counted 7. That is the second-best week of the eight shown for both the count and the cost per lead.
  • What to look into. The returning-visitor ad took $90.17 of the $146.42 and brought 5 of the 7 Meta leads at $18.03 each. Two ads that spent last week, an October video and a September image, show no spend this week and no pause on record, so worth confirming they were turned off on purpose.
  • What is next. We recommend keeping the budget where it is. The most useful next step is on the clinic side: a count of how many of the 6 booked a consultation, so the next report can judge the ads on bookings and not only on form fills.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The returning-visitor ad (5 of the 7 Meta leads on $90.17) opens 'We're Looking for 15 People Who've Been Thinking About Tox' and offers tox at $9.50 per unit plus a free consultation and skin analysis. The video and the model-photo image got 1 lead each, both opening 'Smoother Skin Starts with One Appointment'. Worth testing a first-visit video built on the 'haven't been sure where to start' line: the ad that speaks to hesitation is winning and the cold ads all open the same way.

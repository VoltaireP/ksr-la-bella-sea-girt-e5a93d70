This week at a glance

  • How things are going. 5 leads reached the CRM this week at $44.27 each, down from 9 at $18.61 the week before, on $221.33 of spend. Meta counted 6.
  • What to look into. Cost per CRM lead more than doubled while spend went up. The new event ad with a surprise gift for early bookings spent $43.78 with no leads, and one of the new Sculptra ads was not approved by Meta.
  • What is next. We recommend moving more of the event budget to the ad that opens on the pellet specialist, since it brought 5 of the 6 Meta leads at $16.77 each, and checking how many event leads have booked a time slot.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The ad that opens 'Hormone pellet specialists do not usually sit in a treatment room for five hours answering questions' brought 5 of the 6 Meta leads at $16.77 each. It sells the event as one afternoon of real answers, with same-day bloodwork, a gift card raffle and a free consult for early bookings. The 'Your wellness questions deserve real answers' ad brought 1 on $67.83. Worth testing a second ad built on the specialist's time and access, since that angle beat the perks-first ads this week.

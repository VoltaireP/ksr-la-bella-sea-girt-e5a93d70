This week at a glance

  • How things are going. 7 leads reached the CRM this week at $19.79 each, compared with 4 at $35.63 the week before, on $138.50 of spend. At this budget the weekly count has moved between 3 and 8 all summer, so one week up or down is not yet a trend.
  • What to look into. Every Meta lead matched a CRM lead, so tracking is clean. The proven image ad brought 5 of the 7 leads on $50.22, while the newer image ads took over half the spend for 1 lead. We recommend checking how many of the 7 booked, since bookings are not tracked here yet.
  • What is next. Next we will shift spend back toward the proven image ad and give the newer images a smaller share, then watch whether the cost per lead holds near this week's level for a second week before recommending more budget.
  • Creative ideas to consider. All five ads sell one offer: tox at $9.50 a unit with a free consultation and skin analysis, 15 spots. The proven image set, 'Smoother skin starts with one appointment,' brought 5 of the 7 leads on $50.22. The new images with a second opener, 'Sea Girt: we're looking for 15 people thinking about tox,' brought 1 lead on $40.47, and that opener alone brought none. Next we will test a short video of the consultation under the 'smoother skin' opener, to show the free visit.

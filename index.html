This week at a glance

  • How things are going. 9 leads reached the CRM this week at $18.61 each, up from 2 at $67.36 the week before, on $167.46 of spend. Meta counted 11. Most of the CRM leads came from the new wellness event campaign.
  • What to look into. Meta counted 11 leads but 9 reached the CRM, so worth checking whether the other 2 came through. The tox offer campaign was switched off on Nov 2 and still spent $94.56 this week, likely before the switch.
  • What is next. We recommend keeping the wellness event campaign running up to the Nov 17 event and checking how many of its leads book a time slot, since booked visits matter more than form fills here.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The event ad that opens 'Your wellness questions deserve real answers' brought 7 of the 11 Meta leads on $63.82. It invites people to the Nov 17 hormones and wellness event with a free consult, same-day blood work and a gift card giveaway. The returning-visitor tox ad brought 3 on $57.49 with tox at $9.50 per unit and a free consultation. Worth testing a second event ad that also opens on getting real answers, since that opening brought the most leads at the lowest cost.

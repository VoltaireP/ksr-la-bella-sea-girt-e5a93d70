This week at a glance

  • How things are going. 4 leads reached the CRM this week at $34.26 each, up from 3 at $45.34 the week before, on $137.03 of spend. Meta counted 5. At this budget the weekly count has stayed between 3 and 8 for eight weeks, so one week up is not yet a trend.
  • What to look into. The returning-visitor ad took $86.04 of the $137.03 and brought 3 of the 5 Meta leads. The video launched Oct 2 spent $15.80 and the two image ads $9.10 and $5.10 with no leads between them, so the new video is the one to watch in its second week.
  • What is next. We recommend keeping the budget where it is and giving the Oct 2 video one more week. If it still has no leads by then, worth turning it off and letting that spend flow to the first video ad, which is bringing leads at $9.44 each.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The returning-visitor ad brought 3 of the 5 Meta leads on $86.04. It opens 'We're Looking for 15 People Who've Been Thinking About Tox', offers tox at $9.50 per unit with a free consultation and skin analysis, and promises 'No pressure. No guesswork.' The first video ad brought 2 leads on $18.87, the lowest cost per lead, on the same offer. Worth testing a short video for first-timers who 'haven't been sure where to start', since video earns the cheapest leads and all five ads open the same way.
